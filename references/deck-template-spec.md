# Deck template spec

Master file: `assets/house-template.pptx`. Logo: `assets/rpc-logo.png`. Conversion: skill `pptx-to-google-slides`.

## Canvas

- Widescreen 13.333 in × 7.5 in (16:9)
- Margins 0.55 in
- Type: Calibri / Arial. Cover wordmark is the RPC PNG, not live text
- Fill: `#FFFFFF` field, `#0A0A0A` ink, `#6B7280` secondary, `#B91C1C` accent (sparse)
- Hairlines 0.75 pt `#0A0A0A`

## Chrome

**Cover (every deck):** full white. RPC logo centered. Optional small-caps ticker + date under the mark. No header, no confidential strip, no slide number.

**Interior:** takeaway line under a top hairline. Slide number bottom-left. `Proprietary & Confidential` + `Copyright © {year}. All rights reserved.` bottom-right. Small RPC logo top-right. Never compete with the cover lockup.

## 10-slide research map

Football field is the valuation spine — do not expand to one slide per method on a 10-pager.

1. Cover (white RPC)
2. Agenda
3. Business / model (three-up)
4. Thesis pillars (claim + kill)
5. Financial snapshot (KPI table)
6. **Football field** (all live bars; circular dashed)
7. Load-bearing method (usually DCF)
8. Risks + killing conditions (01–04)
9. Catalysts (roadmap)
10. Recommendation = memo rating + targets + disclaimer

## Conversion

Author PPTX from this master. Upload with Drive `create_file`, conversion on. Never Zapier.
