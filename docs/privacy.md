# Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026.

This policy covers the Equities Valuation plugin published by Reef Point Capital. The plugin is draft research software. It is not an investment adviser.

## What we collect

Reef Point does not operate an account system for this plugin. We do not ask for your name, email, brokerage login, or payment card. We do not sell personal information. We do not use plugin prompts for advertising. We do not run analytics on your research sessions.

The plugin runs in your Codex, ChatGPT, Cursor, or similar host. That host's privacy policy covers what the host stores. We do not receive a copy of your prompts, filings, or generated pages.

## EDGAR

When you pull filings, edgartools runs on your machine and sends your `EDGAR_IDENTITY` (a name and email you set) to the SEC. The SEC requires that identity for fair-access use of EDGAR. Reef Point does not receive it and does not store it.

Public filings and market prices used in a report are public information.

## These pages

Support, privacy, and terms are files in the public GitHub repository. Viewing them on GitHub is covered by GitHub's privacy policy. We do not add our own cookies or tracking pixels to these pages.
