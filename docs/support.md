# Support

Equities Valuation is a Reef Point Capital plugin for draft research on one public company. It is not investment advice, and it is not a brokerage or advisory service.

## Get help

Open an issue on the public repo:

https://github.com/JohnnyUtah7/reef-point-equities-analyst/issues

Include the ticker, the plugin version, and what you expected the page or memo to show. Do not include account passwords, API keys, or brokerage credentials.

There is no phone line and no service-level agreement.

## What you need locally

Filings are pulled with edgartools from your own session. Set `EDGAR_IDENTITY` to your name and email. The SEC requires that identity on requests to EDGAR. The plugin does not send that identity to Reef Point.

## What this plugin does not do

It does not place trades, hold customer accounts, or collect a fee inside the plugin. Output is draft research. You decide what to do with it.
